# The release test reads in short lines, one section at a time

## Goal

The page Edwin reads while testing a release by hand becomes short enough to use at the bench. Your Trainer's v2.2.0 Android sheet has 86 owed checks spread over 353 steps and 37,254 words. The approved example shows one section, the Equipment Hub, as 28 checks in about 1,000 words.

Each section shows three things, in this order:

1. What changed since the previous release, on this platform only, grouped by screen, with before and after screenshots.
2. The setup, folded away: things on the bench, numbered steps before you start, and needs of a single check listed under "Later".
3. The checks, in groups. Each group has a heading and a start state written once. Each check is a number, one action line, one expected line and its TST tag.

The decisions behind this phase are in [[ADR-0050-The-Walk-Becomes-The-Release-Test-And-Its-Expected-Results-Live-In-The-Checks|ADR-0050]]. The work is [[FEAT-0040-The-Release-Test-Reads-In-Short-Lines|FEAT-0040]].

## Scope

- The template's generator produces the new page shape for each section and platform.
- Test notes carry short, single-platform Expect lines.
- Change notes declare their platforms, and each section gets its own "what changed" lines.
- The vocabulary changes everywhere: walk becomes release test, sitting becomes section, survey becomes "what changed".
- The validator warns when a line or a section is too long, and later refuses it.
- A release-preparation skill lets an agent write the short lines and runs the length check.

## Out of Scope

- The page itself. It is built in project-os-cockpit: the release test page in the Tests pane (ID to follow).
- Rewriting Your Trainer's procedures and test notes. That is your-trainer: rewrite of the procedures and test notes, Equipment section pilot (ID to follow).
- Rewriting closed ADRs, change notes and archived notes. They keep the old words.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] The Equipment section of Your Trainer's Android release test, generated by the template, matches the approved example's shape and passes the length check.
- [ ] Every Android section in Your Trainer passes the length check.
- [ ] Every iOS section in Your Trainer passes the length check.
- [ ] The length check is an error, not a warning, in the template.
- [ ] No file outside history (closed ADRs, change notes, archived notes) in the template, project-os-cockpit or your-trainer uses "walk", "sitting" or "survey" for this feature.

## Notes

- Rollout order: pilot the Equipment section end to end in Your Trainer (Android), then the other Android sections, then iOS, then turn the warning into an error.
- Complexity by blast radius: High overall, because the rename touches three repositories and every acceptance check's Expect lines. The pilot is Medium.
- Work lands in three repositories. The template (`~/Dev/repos/project-os`) is planned here. The other two are named above and will get their own IDs.
